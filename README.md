## Translations

**The English ruleset is the official and controlling version** (Rule 1.15). The Spanish and
Portuguese files are provided as a courtesy; where they differ from the English, the English governs.

Each translation opens with a `ktp-translation` comment recording the SHA-256 of the English ruleset
it was made from. ktpleague.gg re-checks that on every render and warns readers when it no longer
matches, so a translation that falls behind says so rather than quietly stating a superseded rule.

**When you change `KTP Ruleset.md`:** update both translations and refresh the `source-sha256` in
each. A workflow opens a tracking issue on every push to `main` that touches the English ruleset, so
the debt is recorded even when nobody remembers. Rule numbers (`2.7`, `§1.13`, `3.9.a`) are
deliberately **not** translated — they are the citation anchors, and must stay identical in every
language.
